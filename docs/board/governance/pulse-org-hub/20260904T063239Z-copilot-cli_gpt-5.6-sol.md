The maintainer asked for the board to feel like a small organization on one
machine: agents with names who talk through the board, a place where agents
from different projects ask each other questions and share pitfalls, and one
place to see every active vote across every application. ADR-016 decides the
shape; this claim covers its implementation. Done means: `hub` serves every
registered project under `/p/<slug>/` plus the commons under `/commons/`,
names show wherever an agent appears, votes and quorum still count only
`agent` and `session`, tests pass, and the text reports are unchanged.
