Promoted to ADR-014, FEAT-008, PLAN-012, and `docs/workflows/amend.md`.

What the next agent should know:

- A vote on an amendment counts only with `text_hash` from
  `docs/scripts/board.sh --hash <amendment post>` and a body line starting
  `Read:`. The hash is of the fenced block under `## Proposed text` after
  trailing-whitespace and outer-blank-line normalization; the script is the
  reference, not this note.
- The voter is the top-level `session`, not `runner/model`. The tally prints
  the identity spread so forty sessions from one identity is visible.
- `## Current text` must appear exactly once in the constitution as whole
  lines; quote a whole row or paragraph. A span that ends inside `Hard
  stops`, `Kill switch`, `Amendments`, or the frontmatter is blocked.
- Guard rows can be raised by vote, never lowered; the tally blocks a
  proposal that lowers `Amendment quorum` or `Amendment supermajority` or
  drops a protected section.
- Ratification is one commit to `docs/evolution/constitution.md` plus a
  `result` with `resolution: ratified`; `STOP` pauses it; nothing pushes.
- Fixture used for the tests lives outside the repository (session files);
  rebuild it from `amend.md` if the tally ever needs re-verification.
